The official release reports completion of a three-day 2026 training on AI-based system and database security. It states that 42 Korbrimob personnel participated, including personnel from Mako staff, Gegana, Pelopor, Satintel, and Satlat.

## Use in the Background
This source is implementation evidence that technology capability development has moved beyond the Renstra's strategic wording into a concrete 2026 personnel-development activity across several Korbrimob components.

## Evidentiary boundary
This source does **not** establish Digital Job Demands, technostress, burnout, POS, or resilience levels. Training participation cannot be treated as evidence that technology is harmful or that personnel experience high digital demands.
