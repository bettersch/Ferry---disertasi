| Relevance | Provides a direct theoretical bridge from Korbrimob digital modernization to a candidate employee-level demand construct without equating technology use with stress/burnout. | Conceptual model |

## Critical appraisal
The source directly supports DJD as a candidate construct within JD-R but is expert/white-collar evidence and does not establish relevance or prevalence for Korbrimob. Digitalization can produce both demands and resources.
