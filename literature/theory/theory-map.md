# Theory Map and Contribution Audit — Ferry Stage 2

Status/version: UNDER REVIEW v0.1, 2026-10-11. Search parameter set: `PRM-001`. Search runs: `RUN-001`–`RUN-005`. Issue: #5.

## Grand theory

- Theory: **Job Demands–Resources (JD-R) Theory — PROPOSED as the primary explanatory theory, pending researcher verification of GRO-02.**
- Original source: `SRC-002` (Demerouti et al., 2001).
- Current development sources inspected: `SRC-003` (Bakker, Demerouti, & Sanz-Vergel, 2023); `SRC-004` and `SRC-005` (Scholze & Hecker digital extension).
- Role at Stage 2: **explanatory lens** for how work characteristics are organized as demands/resources and how a health-impairment process can culminate in burnout. Whether it later becomes a formal hypothesis generator depends on Stage 4 research questions and the final paradigm/design.

### Why JD-R is currently the most parsimonious candidate

1. The original theory directly organizes work characteristics into job demands and job resources and links them differentially to burnout (`SRC-002`).
2. Contemporary JD-R explicitly includes multilevel developments while retaining burnout/work engagement as focal outcomes (`SRC-003`).
3. Digital-work research has explicitly extended JD-R to Digital Job Demands and Digital Job Resources (`SRC-004`, `SRC-005`), fitting the verified Korbrimob modernization phenomenon without treating technology as inherently harmful.
4. COR (`SRC-009`) offers a compatible resource-loss mechanism but, on evidence inspected so far, does not provide a necessary organizing function that JD-R lacks for the candidate employee demand/resource → burnout process.
5. Organizational Support Theory (`SRC-006`, `SRC-007`) is necessary to define/explain POS specifically, but it does not organize Digital Job Demands, burnout, and organizational resilience as a whole.
6. Duchek's capability/process framework (`SRC-008`) is necessary to define the organizational-resilience outcome domain but does not supply the employee health-impairment mechanism.

## Concepts and debates

| Concept/position | Level | Function | Main evidence | Contradictions/limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Job demands/resources | primarily employee/job, with later multilevel extensions | organize work conditions and strain/motivation mechanisms | `SRC-002`, `SRC-003` | flexible theory requires construct-specific justification; it does not validate every candidate path |
| Digital Job Demands | employee/job characteristic | digitalized-work demand exposure | `SRC-004`, `SRC-005` | white-collar origin; digital intensity is not automatically demand |
| POS | employee perception of organization | candidate organizational/job resource | `SRC-006`, `SRC-007` | not identical to supervisor/coworker/objective support |
| Burnout | employee health-impairment condition | candidate downstream mechanism | `SRC-002`, `SRC-003` plus Ferry Burnout matrix | measurement and causal ordering remain separate issues |
| Organizational resilience | organizational capability/process | candidate downstream outcome domain | `SRC-008` | level-of-analysis mismatch if measured only as individual perception |
| COR | individual/resource stress theory | competing/complementary resource-loss explanation | `SRC-009` | broader and potentially redundant for the core demand/resource→burnout process |

## Theory-selection limits

- No Scopus/Web of Science theory-development run was executed in this session; `PRM-001` is targeted verification only.
- The four Ferry matrices were inspected as working artifacts but their internal `PASS` statements are not repository gate assessments.
- `GAP-001` is an unresolved theoretical bridge, not proof of novelty.
- `GAP-002` is a boundary/application question and must not be converted into novelty merely because Korbrimob has not been studied.
- Method, measurement, hypotheses, and causal ordering remain outside this theory-map decision.
