## Critical appraisal
Duchek is conceptually strong for defining the organizational-resilience domain but does not establish that individual burnout causes organizational resilience. Using it downstream of person-level JD-R therefore requires an explicit cross-level mechanism or narrower employee-rated/perceived outcome.
