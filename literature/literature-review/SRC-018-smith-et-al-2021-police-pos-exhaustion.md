## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Two-wave police survey with a three-month lag; 281 active-duty Canadian police officers. |
| Constructs | POS; officer voice; organizational cynicism; stress; emotional exhaustion. |
| Findings | POS predicted subsequent variance in cynicism, stress, and emotional exhaustion; voice moderated cynicism but not as a universal protective mechanism. |
| Relevance | Strong police-specific evidence that employee-perceived organizational support is associated with later strain/exhaustion. |

## Critical appraisal
Temporal separation is stronger than single-wave surveys, but the study remains observational/self-report and emotional exhaustion is narrower than a full burnout syndrome.

## Statement relations
Supports Stage 3 inference that POS→burnout/exhaustion evidence is comparatively strong in policing, while limiting any claim that support always buffers every stress pathway.
