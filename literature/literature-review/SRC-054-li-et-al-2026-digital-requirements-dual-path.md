# SRC-054 — Li et al. (2026), digital technology requirements as challenge/hindrance demands

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-054
- Track: literature-review (Stage 3)
- Found by: `RUN-035`; parameter set `PRM-005`; screened with `PRM-003`
- Source type: peer-reviewed three-wave empirical article
- Metadata: Li, S., Wang, Y., & Wang, X. (2026). To embrace or to avoid: the dual path effects of digital technology requirements on employees with different digital literacy. *Frontiers in Psychology*.
- DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1676932
- Access: FULL_TEXT.

## Extraction
Three-wave data from 332 employees undergoing digital transformation showed digital technology requirements could trigger different behavioral paths depending on whether they were appraised as challenge or hindrance demands and on digital literacy.

## Critical appraisal
Strong temporal evidence for a dual-path digital-demand boundary, but not policing and not the exact Scholze–Hecker DJD measure or Adaptive Performance outcome.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS challenge/hindrance qualification of digital demands; LIMITS a universally negative DJD→performance hypothesis.
