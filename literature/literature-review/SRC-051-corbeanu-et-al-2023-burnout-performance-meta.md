- DOI: 10.1080/1359432X.2023.2209320

## Extraction
Across 45 organizational studies, random-effects correlations were approximately −.17 for exhaustion, −.16 for depersonalization and −.23 for inefficacy with job performance, with moderation by burnout measure and occupation for several dimensions.

## Critical appraisal
Strong synthesis supporting a modest adverse burnout–performance association, not a large deterministic effect. The performance criterion is broader than Adaptive Performance, and meta-analytic association does not identify mediation or causal direction.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS modest negative Burnout–performance association; LIMITS exaggerated performance claims; DOES NOT prove Burnout mediation to Adaptive Performance.
