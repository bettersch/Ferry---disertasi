## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Cross-sectional survey; 249 working professionals. |
| Constructs | Individual resilience; work engagement; burnout; performance. |
| Findings | Resilience was negatively related to burnout and positively related to engagement/performance; burnout was not significantly related to performance, whereas engagement was. |
| Directionality relevance | Directly cautions against assuming that burnout necessarily transmits resilience-related effects to downstream performance outcomes. |

## Critical appraisal
Individual resilience and generic performance are not POR/organizational resilience, and cross-sectional evidence cannot identify causal direction. Its value is as a null counterexample to an automatic burnout-to-performance bridge.

## Statement relations
LIMITS generalized claims that Burnout necessarily predicts adaptive/performance outcomes; supports retaining mixed evidence in the downstream mechanism audit.
