## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Cross-sectional survey; 384 faculty/administrative employees in educational institutions; moderated-mediation analysis. |
| Constructs | Emotional exhaustion; organizational citizenship behavior; task performance; emotional intelligence; leadership style. |
| Findings | OCB statistically mediated the exhaustion–performance relation, with leadership and emotional intelligence conditioning parts of the process. |
| Directionality relevance | Shows one plausible micro-behavior pathway from exhaustion toward performance-relevant outcomes, but not toward POR or validated organizational-resilience capability. |

## Critical appraisal
Same-source/cross-sectional mediation is not causal mediation, and performance is not resilience. The study supports only a possible micro-behavior mechanism that would require separate theory, measurement and temporal identification.

## Statement relations
CONTEXTUALIZES a possible Burnout → behavior/performance mechanism; DOES NOT SUPPORT the original Burnout → POR mediation claim.
