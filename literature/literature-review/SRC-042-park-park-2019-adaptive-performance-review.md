## Extraction
The review synthesized 34 empirical studies on adaptive performance and identified antecedents at individual, job, group and organizational levels. It treats adaptive performance as an employee performance domain concerning effective adjustment of behavior to changing work requirements, rather than as a perception of organizational capability.

## Critical appraisal
Strong construct-boundary evidence and a mature literature base. It does not establish the exact Ferry antecedent set or policing-specific effects.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS adaptive performance as a distinct person-level behavioral outcome; SUPPORTS multi-level antecedent logic; DOES NOT establish DJD/POS→Burnout→adaptive-performance mediation.
