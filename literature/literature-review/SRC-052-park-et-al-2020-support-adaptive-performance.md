# SRC-052 — Park et al. (2020), organizational support and Adaptive Performance

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-052
- Track: literature-review (Stage 3)
- Found by: `RUN-030` / `RUN-034`; parameter set `PRM-005`; screened with `PRM-003`
- Source type: peer-reviewed empirical article
- Metadata: Park, Y., Lim, D. H., Kim, W., & Kang, H. (2020). Organizational Support and Adaptive Performance: The Revolving Structural Relationships between Job Crafting, Work Engagement, and Adaptive Performance. *Sustainability, 12*(12), 4872.
- DOI: 10.3390/su12124872
- Access: FULL_TEXT.

## Extraction
Using 250 HR professionals in South Korea and SEM, organizational support was linked to Adaptive Performance through job crafting and work engagement, and the study emphasized reciprocal/revolving relationships among crafting, engagement and Adaptive Performance.

## Critical appraisal
Direct Adaptive Performance evidence and a plausible resource mechanism, but non-police and cross-sectional structural modeling. Importantly, the mechanism is motivational/job-crafting rather than burnout.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS organizational support–Adaptive Performance relation; SUPPORTS engagement/job-crafting rival mechanism; LIMITS a burnout-only mechanism.
