- DOI: 10.20473/jmtt.v17i3.54856

## Extraction
A cross-sectional multisector sample of 230 employees found POS positively associated with Adaptive Performance both directly and indirectly via role-breadth self-efficacy.

## Critical appraisal
Direct construct match for POS and Adaptive Performance and useful Indonesian-region evidence, but not police-specific and cross-sectional. It indicates alternative self-efficacy mechanisms rather than a burnout pathway.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS POS–Adaptive Performance association; SUPPORTS self-efficacy rival mechanism; DOES NOT establish causality.
