## Extraction
The study adapted the Adaptive Performance Scale of Charbonnier-Voirin & Roussel for Indonesia using translation/content review and CFA on 322 employees. The reported model supports a reliable multidimensional five-factor adaptive-performance construct.

## Critical appraisal
This is highly relevant measurement evidence because it reduces language/context adaptation risk for an Indonesian person-level outcome. It is not police-specific and does not establish construct prevalence or causal relations in Korbrimob.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS measurement maturity and Indonesian feasibility of adaptive performance; LIMITS the need to invent a new outcome instrument; DOES NOT support a final structural path.
