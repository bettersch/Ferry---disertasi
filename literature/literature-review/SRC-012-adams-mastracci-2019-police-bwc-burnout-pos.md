## Extraction
The study places a specific policing technology—body-worn cameras—at the center of officer well-being and organizational-support perceptions. In the reported SEM, BWC use was associated with higher burnout and lower POS, with POS included in the pathway between BWC use and burnout.

## Use in the Background
Provides direct police-specific evidence that technology implementation can alter the employee experience of work and may relate to both strain and support perceptions. It is a comparator for Ferry's digital-work framing, not evidence that all police technologies or Korbrimob digitalization have the same effects.

## Evidentiary boundary
- BWC exposure is not equivalent to the broader Digital Job Demands construct.
- The study is observational and does not establish a general causal effect of digitalization.
- Results from U.S. patrol officers cannot be treated as prevalence/effect estimates for Korbrimob.
