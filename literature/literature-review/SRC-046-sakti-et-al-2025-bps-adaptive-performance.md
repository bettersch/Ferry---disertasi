## Extraction
The study surveyed 510 team leaders in BPS-Statistics Indonesia and examined work overload, information-technology use, readiness to change and adaptive performance, including job-satisfaction mediation. It provides Indonesian public-sector evidence that adaptive performance can be modeled as an employee-level response in a technology/change context.

## Critical appraisal
Strong contextual comparator for a modernization/public-sector setting, but not policing and not evidence for Ferry's exact DJD/POS/Burnout architecture. Its cross-sectional structural model should not be read causally.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS Indonesian public-sector relevance of adaptive performance; CONTEXTUALIZES technology/workload and adaptation; DOES NOT establish Ferry's causal paths.
