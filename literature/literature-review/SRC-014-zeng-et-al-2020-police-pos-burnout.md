# SRC-014 — Zeng et al. (2020), POS and police job burnout

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-014
- Track: targeted empirical evidence for Issue #26
- Source type: peer-reviewed open-access journal article
- Metadata: Zeng, X., Zhang, X., Chen, M., Liu, J., & Wu, C. (2020). The influence of perceived organizational support on police job burnout: A moderated mediation model. *Frontiers in Psychology, 11*, 948.
- DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2020.00948
- Sample: 784 police officers
- Retrieval: publisher full text and Elicit, 2026-10-11
- Access: FULL_TEXT

## Extraction
The study reports a negative association between POS and police burnout (reported r = -.42; adjusted regression coefficient beta = -.42). Job satisfaction partially mediated the POS-burnout association, while regulatory emotional self-efficacy conditioned part of the indirect process.

## Use in the Background
Provides direct police-specific empirical support for treating POS as a plausible employee-perceived resource related to burnout.

## Evidentiary boundary
- Cross-sectional/self-report evidence does not establish causal direction.
- The study does not show that POS is low or burnout is high in Korbrimob.
- POS should remain distinct from supervisor support, objective resources, and welfare provision.
