## Extraction
The article examines organizational support, supervisor justice, technology efficacy/difficulty, job satisfaction, and occupational stress among Chinese police officers. The reported model indicates that technology-related difficulty is part of the pathway linking organizational conditions to employee well-being outcomes.

## Use in the Background
This provides police-specific evidence that the consequences of digital technology depend on how technology is experienced and supported, rather than on technology use alone. It helps distinguish a bounded digital-work demand from generic digitalization.

## Evidentiary boundary
- Technology difficulty is related to, but not identical with, Ferry's Digital Job Demands construct.
- The study does not establish effects in Korbrimob.
- The design does not eliminate reverse causality or common-method concerns.
