# SRC-050 — Gomes et al. (2022), burnout and performance in Portuguese police

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-050
- Track: literature-review (Stage 3)
- Found by: `RUN-032`; parameter set `PRM-005`; screened with `PRM-003`
- Source type: peer-reviewed police empirical article
- Metadata: Gomes, G. P., Ribeiro, N., & Gomes, D. R. (2022). The Impact of Burnout on Police Officers’ Performance and Turnover Intention: The Moderating Role of Compassion Satisfaction. *Administrative Sciences, 12*(3), 92.
- DOI: 10.3390/admsci12030092
- Access: FULL_TEXT.

## Extraction
Survey data from 1,682 Portuguese police officers showed a negative association between burnout and job performance and a positive association with turnover intention; compassion satisfaction did not moderate the burnout–performance relation.

## Critical appraisal
Large direct police sample and useful performance evidence, but cross-sectional/self-report and general performance rather than Adaptive Performance. Causal wording in the article should not be imported into Ferry's claims.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS negative Burnout–performance association in policing; QUALIFIES but does not establish Burnout→Adaptive Performance.
