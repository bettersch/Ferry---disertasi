- DOI: 10.1177/00218863231165785
- Access: ABSTRACT_ONLY in this run

## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Mixed-method multi-study in the Pakistani textile industry; includes longitudinal survey data during an ongoing crisis. |
| Constructs | Organizational resilience; employee-focused/learning facilitators; emotional exhaustion; business success. |
| Findings | Organizational resilience was associated with lower emotional exhaustion and better business outcomes; employee focus and learning orientation were important facilitators. |
| Relevance | Critical reverse-order evidence: resilience capability may reduce employee exhaustion, not only emerge downstream from employee health. |

## Critical appraisal
Private-sector crisis context differs from policing, but the longitudinal component materially challenges a one-directional Burnout→OR assumption.

## Statement relations
CONTRADICTS/LIMITS any unqualified downstream ordering from burnout to resilience and supports reciprocal or reverse-direction alternatives.
