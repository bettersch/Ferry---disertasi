# SRC-016 — Giovannini & Giauque (2024), organizational resilience in police/prisons

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-016
- Track: targeted empirical evidence for Issue #26
- Source type: peer-reviewed open-access journal article
- Metadata: Giovannini, C., & Giauque, D. (2024). Key factors of organizational resilience in prisons and police forces in French-speaking Switzerland during COVID-19. *Merits, 4*(1), 51–65.
- DOI: 10.3390/merits4010004
- Design: qualitative; 25 semi-structured interviews with executives across cantonal police and prison institutions
- Retrieval: publisher/open full text and Elicit, 2026-10-11
- Access: FULL_TEXT

## Extraction
The study identifies resilience-related levers across anticipation, response, and post-shock learning in police and prison organizations, including proactive culture, information management and communication, leadership, social/environmental practices, governance agility, resourcefulness, and learning capabilities.

## Use in the Background
Provides a close public-safety comparator showing that organizational resilience in policing is fundamentally an organizational capability/process issue involving governance, information, leadership, resources, and learning—not simply an employee psychological state.

## Evidentiary boundary
- Retrospective qualitative evidence from Swiss police/prisons cannot establish Korbrimob resilience levels.
- The study does not test burnout as a mediator or causal antecedent of organizational resilience.
- It supports retaining leadership, resources, governance, and learning as rival/common-cause explanations.
