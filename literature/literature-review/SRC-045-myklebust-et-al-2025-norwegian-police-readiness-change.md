- DOI: 10.1177/14613557251337304
- Access: ABSTRACT/METADATA in current run.

## Extraction
A survey of 622 Norwegian Police Service employees tested organizational climates and individual readiness for change. The preferred model indicated that a learning climate mediated the relation between a broader human-relations climate and individual readiness for change.

## Critical appraisal
Direct police evidence and useful construct-boundary evidence. The outcome is individual readiness for change, not perceived organizational resilience or operational readiness; its antecedents also underscore the importance of organizational climate/resources.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS police relevance of readiness; LIMITS construct equivalence between readiness and resilience; SUPPORTS organizational-context antecedent alternative.
