## Extraction
Panel/public-sector evidence links employee-perceived organizational strategic agility with intention to stay over time and identifies trust in leadership as a contributor. Together with the authors' earlier 2023 work (`SRC-029`), agility is empirically positioned as an organizational condition/resource experienced by employees rather than as a natural downstream consequence of burnout.

## Critical appraisal
Good public-sector and temporal relevance, but not policing-specific. It directly cautions against selecting agility as Y merely because it is measurable: its nomological position is often upstream of employee outcomes.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS agility measurement/public-sector relevance; SUPPORTS upstream-resource interpretation; LIMITS agility as Ferry's downstream Y.
