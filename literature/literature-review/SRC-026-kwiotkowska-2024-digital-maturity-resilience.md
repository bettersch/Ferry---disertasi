## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | fsQCA study of Polish combined heat and power plants. |
| Constructs | Digital maturity/intensity; transformation management intensity; dynamic capabilities; organizational resilience. |
| Findings | High organizational resilience was achieved through more than one configuration; digital maturity and dynamic capabilities appeared in alternative/equifinal pathways. |
| Relevance | Demonstrates that resilience is configurational and that no single digital factor is necessary or sufficient. |

## Critical appraisal
Sector-specific organizational-level evidence; fsQCA identifies configurations rather than average net causal effects. It does not address employee burnout.

## Statement relations
LIMITS any simple one-variable explanation of organizational resilience and SUPPORTS retaining dynamic capabilities/management intensity as serious rival mechanisms.
