# SRC-049 — McCarthy et al. (2016), emotional exhaustion and police job performance

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-049
- Track: literature-review (Stage 3)
- Found by: `RUN-032`; parameter set `PRM-005`; screened with `PRM-003`
- Source type: peer-reviewed three-wave police study
- Metadata: McCarthy, J. M., Trougakos, J. P., & Cheng, B. H. (2016). Are anxious workers less productive workers? It depends on the quality of social exchange. *Journal of Applied Psychology*.
- DOI: 10.1037/apl0000044
- Access: repository/full-text link returned by Elicit.

## Extraction
A three-wave study of police officers found emotional exhaustion explained part of the relation between workplace anxiety and job performance, while coworker/leader exchange conditioned parts of the resource-depletion/performance process.

## Critical appraisal
Strong temporal and locus relevance for the proposition that resource depletion/exhaustion can matter for police performance. The outcome is general job performance, not Adaptive Performance, and exhaustion is narrower than full burnout.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS a qualified burnout/exhaustion–performance bridge in policing; CONTEXTUALIZES social-resource moderation; DOES NOT establish Burnout→Adaptive Performance mediation.
