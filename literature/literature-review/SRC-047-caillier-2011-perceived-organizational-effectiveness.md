- Access: ABSTRACT/METADATA in current run.

## Extraction
Survey data from 330 U.S. government workers were used to model perceived organizational effectiveness. Employee perceptions of funding, role ambiguity, participation in decisions, job performance and public-service motivation were among the relevant predictors.

## Critical appraisal
Shows feasibility of employee-rated organizational effectiveness but also demonstrates its breadth: the construct can absorb multiple management, resource and individual evaluations. That breadth reduces construct specificity for Ferry's adaptation puzzle and heightens overlap/common-method risk.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS feasibility of perceived organizational effectiveness; LIMITS it as a specific adaptation outcome; FLAGS overlap with resource/support conditions.
