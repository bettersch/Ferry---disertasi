- Access: FULL_TEXT via institutional repository link returned by Elicit.

## Extraction
A one-year follow-up among 580 police officers examined resources measured before organizational change and adaptivity during change. Leader–member exchange and personal resources were positively related, and baseline resources predicted later adaptivity.

## Critical appraisal
Strong direct policing and temporal evidence that resources can facilitate adaptation. It concerns organizational change broadly, not digital modernization; LMX and personal resources are not POS.

## Statement relations
LIMITS claims that resource→adaptivity is novel in policing; SUPPORTS treating Ferry's contribution as a digital-domain and construct-boundary extension rather than invention of a police adaptation mechanism.
