## Extraction
The study integrates a systematic literature review and expert interviews to extend JD-R to digital work, distinguishing digital job demands and digital job resources and emphasizing that digitization can create both burdens and resources depending on work context.

## Critical appraisal
Useful construct-domain foundation and direct digital JD-R framing, but not policing and not an outcome-specific test of Adaptive Performance. The broad concept of DJD therefore cannot itself be claimed as novel in Ferry.

## Statement relations
LIMITS novelty claims based on merely introducing DJD; SUPPORTS a context-specific construct-boundary contribution and asymmetric health/performance testing.
