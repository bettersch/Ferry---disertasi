## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Cross-sectional wellness survey of 167 sworn officers in a medium-sized police department. |
| Constructs | Police work stress; organizational support; community support; burnout; life satisfaction. |
| Findings | Organizational support moderated work-stress associations with burnout and life satisfaction; community support did not show the same moderation. |
| Relevance | Direct policing evidence that organizational support can condition work-stress/burnout relations. |

## Critical appraisal
The moderation finding is context-specific and based on same-wave self-report data; it does not establish a universal POS buffer or causal mitigation effect.

## Statement relations
Supports the relevance of support as a resource in policing and simultaneously limits any claim that all forms of support operate equivalently.
