## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Cross-sectional survey; 672 IT-sector respondents; path analysis. |
| Constructs | Emotional exhaustion; depersonalization; knowledge sharing; work accomplishment; organizational performance. |
| Findings | Emotional exhaustion was negatively related to work accomplishment and organizational-performance ratings; knowledge sharing showed the opposite pattern. |
| Directionality relevance | Provides a plausible downstream consequence of exhaustion for generic performance, but does not test POR, organizational resilience, or adaptive capability. |

## Critical appraisal
Useful as mechanism-adjacent evidence that health impairment may matter for organizationally relevant outcomes, but same-source cross-sectional ratings and a generic performance construct do not identify a Burnout → POR pathway.

## Statement relations
CONTEXTUALIZES possible downstream consequences of exhaustion; DOES NOT SUPPORT direct Burnout → POR mediation.
