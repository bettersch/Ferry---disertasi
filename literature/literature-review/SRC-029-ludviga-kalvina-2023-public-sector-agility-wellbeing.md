## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Cross-sectional employee survey during COVID-19 crisis; public-sector organizations; n=5,469; SEM. |
| Constructs | Perceived strategic agility; organizational learning; leadership; aim clarity; work engagement; well-being. |
| Findings | Perceived strategic agility was positively associated with work engagement and, through engagement, well-being; learning, leadership and aim clarity contributed to agility. |
| Directionality relevance | Places employee-perceived organizational adaptive capability upstream of employee well-being rather than employee strain upstream of organizational adaptation. |

## Critical appraisal
Large public-sector sample and direct employee-rated organizational-adaptation construct are highly relevant to the POR boundary, but cross-sectional SEM does not identify temporal causality and strategic agility is not identical to organizational resilience.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS reverse/top-down organizational-adaptation → employee-well-being ordering; LIMITS any claim that Burnout → POR is the empirically dominant direction.
