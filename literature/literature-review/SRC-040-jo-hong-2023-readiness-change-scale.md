## Extraction
The study developed and validated a multidimensional organizational-change readiness measure using a pilot sample and a large main sample. It reinforces that readiness is measurable at multiple levels and dimensions, but its object is readiness for organizational change rather than broad organizational capacity to anticipate, cope and adapt across disruptions.

## Critical appraisal
Strong measurement evidence for the readiness candidate. Construct specificity is simultaneously its strength and its limitation for Ferry because the Renstra documents continuing modernization and operational adaptation rather than one bounded change intervention.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS readiness measurement maturity; LIMITS readiness as a universal downstream adaptation outcome.
