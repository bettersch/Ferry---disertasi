## Extraction
The study separates digital learning demands from digital workload and reports divergent downstream processes: learning demands were linked to proactive service behavior through problem-solving pondering, while digital workload was linked to service sabotage through affective rumination; cognitive flexibility conditioned part of the positive pathway.

## Critical appraisal
Strong evidence that the content of a digital demand matters and that aggregating all digital demands into one uniformly harmful exposure can conceal theoretically different processes. The context is hospitality, not policing, and the outcome is service behavior rather than Adaptive Performance.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS a construct-boundary novelty direction and LIMITS any universal negative DJD→performance proposition.
