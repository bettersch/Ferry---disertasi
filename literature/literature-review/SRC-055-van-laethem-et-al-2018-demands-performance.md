# SRC-055 — Van Laethem et al. (2018/2019), challenge/hindrance demands and performance

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-055
- Track: literature-review (Stage 3)
- Found by: `RUN-035`; parameter set `PRM-005`; screened with `PRM-003`
- Source type: peer-reviewed three-wave longitudinal article
- Metadata: Van Laethem, M., Beckers, D. G. J., de Bloom, J., Sianoja, M., & Kinnunen, U. Challenge and hindrance demands in relation to self-reported job performance and the role of restoration, sleep quality, and affective rumination. *Journal of Occupational and Organizational Psychology*.
- DOI: 10.1111/joop.12239
- Access: FULL_TEXT.

## Extraction
Across three waves and 920 Finnish employees, challenge job demands were positively related to later task/contextual performance, whereas the tested hindrance demand did not show the expected longitudinal performance relation.

## Critical appraisal
High-quality boundary evidence demonstrating that demand–performance relations are not uniformly negative. The demands are general, not digital, and the outcome is general performance rather than Adaptive Performance.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS challenge/hindrance qualification; LIMITS a universal negative demand→performance path.
