|---|---|
| Design/context | Cross-cultural scale development/validation using 1,435 employees from 138 organizations in Spain and Austria. |
| Construct | Organisational resilience represented by ability and process dimensions. |
| Measurement evidence | Exploratory and confirmatory analyses supported a two-dimensional structure; reliability, convergent validity and discriminant validity were reported as adequate. |
| GAP-005 relevance | Materially weakens any claim that organizational-resilience measurement through employee surveys is inherently too immature to use. |

## Critical appraisal
Strong measurement-development evidence but not policing-specific. A valid employee-report organizational-resilience scale does not solve the causal-ordering problem, common-method overlap with POS, or whether organizational resilience is the best downstream outcome for Ferry.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS measurement feasibility of employee-rated organizational resilience; LIMITS rejection of POR based solely on measurement immaturity; DOES NOT SUPPORT Burnout→POR.
