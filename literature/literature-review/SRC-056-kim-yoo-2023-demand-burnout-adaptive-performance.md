# SRC-056 — Kim & Yoo (2023), job demands, burnout and Adaptive Performance

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-056

- Source type: peer-reviewed cross-sectional empirical article
- Metadata: Kim, M.-K., & Yoo, T. (2023). The Relationship between Job Demand and Adaptive Performance: Mediating Effect of Burnout and Moderating Effect of Leader-Member Exchange and Emotional Stability. *Dongguk Business Research Institute, 45*(1), 79–114.
- DOI: 10.55685/bcr.2023.45.1.79
- Access: metadata/abstract in current environment.

## Extraction
Survey data from 272 Korean employees tested workload, time pressure, role conflict and role ambiguity, burnout and Adaptive Performance, reporting a statistical burnout mediation pattern and moderation by emotional stability for qualitative demands.

## Critical appraisal
This is an exact adjacent architecture but uses general job demands, not DJD, is cross-sectional, non-police and has limited evidentiary weight for temporal mediation. The positive direct demand–Adaptive Performance relations also underscore challenge/hindrance complexity.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS plausibility of a Burnout–Adaptive Performance bridge; LIMITS mediation lock because temporal identification and construct match are weak.
