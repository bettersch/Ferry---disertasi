# SRC-057 — Chen et al. (2024), organizational support, technology use and police well-being

- DOI: 10.1177/10986111241275043
- Access: FULL_TEXT available through publisher link returned by Elicit.

## Extraction
Survey evidence from Chinese police officers examined organizational support, supervisor justice, technology efficacy/difficulty, job satisfaction and occupational stress. Organizational support was positively related to job satisfaction, while technology difficulty was implicated in stress-related pathways.

## Critical appraisal
Direct policing and digital-technology evidence in a strong field journal. The design is observational and does not test a bounded DJD construct, burnout, or Adaptive Performance.

## Statement relations
LIMITS novelty claims that policing lacks technology-demand/support/well-being research; SUPPORTS the narrower construct-boundary problem around bounded DJD and Adaptive Performance.
