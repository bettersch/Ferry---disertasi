# SRC-061 — Zhou et al. (2025), digital transformation as a double-edged sword for Adaptive Performance

- Metadata: Zhou, Q.-W., Cheng, S., Fu, F.-Q., Zhang, J., & Jiang, Y. (2025). Enterprise digital transformation as a double-edged sword for employees: an investigation based on the Job Demands-Resources model. *Humanities and Social Sciences Communications*.
- DOI: 10.1057/s41599-025-05985-4
- Access: FULL_TEXT.

## Extraction
Three-wave data from 432 employees linked digital technology affordance to divergent employee processes. For learning-goal-oriented employees, job control helped translate digital technology affordance into higher Adaptive Performance; for performance-goal-oriented employees, digital technology affordance increased job demands and perceived status threat.

## Critical appraisal
Directly relevant to Adaptive Performance and a digital JD-R logic, and stronger temporally than cross-sectional evidence. However, the exposure is digital technology affordance rather than bounded DJD, and the context is not policing.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS the double-edged/asymmetric performance logic and LIMITS claims that digitalization or digital demands have one fixed performance direction.
