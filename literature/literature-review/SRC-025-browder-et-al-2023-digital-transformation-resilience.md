## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Two longitudinal case studies of incumbent essential businesses; 72 crisis adaptations analyzed during COVID-19. |
| Constructs | Digital transformation/digital capabilities; crisis adaptation; organizational resilience. |
| Findings | Pre-existing digital capabilities enabled unplanned crisis adaptations and resilience-seeking responses. |
| Relevance | Strong competing mechanism: digital technologies can build organization-level adaptive capacity rather than only create employee demands. |

## Critical appraisal
Private-sector case evidence cannot establish employee-level well-being effects or Brimob transfer, but its longitudinal crisis design is informative for the organizational-resilience side.

## Statement relations
CONTRADICTS a one-sided digitalization→strain narrative and SUPPORTS treating digital capability as a rival positive pathway to true organizational resilience.
