## Extraction
A survey of 346 personnel in an anonymized Institution X under a PRESISI digital-transformation agenda tested role overload, technostress, work engagement, burnout and job performance. Role overload and technostress were associated with both engagement and burnout; engagement was the dominant performance mechanism, while burnout was not a significant mediator.

## Critical appraisal
Highly relevant construct configuration but the source is recent, uncited, and the institution is anonymized in the retrieved metadata. It measures technostress and generic job performance rather than bounded DJD and Adaptive Performance. Causal language is not warranted from the survey design.

## Statement relations
STRONGLY LIMITS any novelty claim based on the mere combination of digital stress, burnout/engagement and performance in an Indonesian public-sector modernization setting; SUPPORTS keeping engagement as a rival mechanism and narrowing Ferry's novelty.
