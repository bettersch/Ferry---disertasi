## Critical appraisal
Conceptually aligned with Ferry's resilience domain, but sample/validation is early and not policing-specific. It does not identify resilience as an employee-health downstream outcome.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS measurement maturity trend for organizational resilience; CONTEXTUALIZES POR; DOES NOT SUPPORT Burnout→POR.
