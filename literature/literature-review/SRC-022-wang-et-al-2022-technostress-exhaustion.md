## Extraction
| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Design/context | Three-wave survey of 235 employees from three manufacturing companies in China. |
| Constructs | Technostress; work exhaustion; employee well-being; workplace knowledge diversity. |
| Findings | Work exhaustion statistically mediated the association between technostress and lower well-being; workplace knowledge diversity buffered the indirect relation. |
| Relevance | Stronger temporal design than single-wave SEM and supports exhaustion as a health-impairment mechanism downstream of digital stressors. |

## Critical appraisal
Manufacturing context limits direct transfer to Brimob, and exhaustion is narrower than full burnout. Three-wave ordering improves but does not fully establish causal identification.

## Statement relations
Supports the broad digital-demand → exhaustion pathway while limiting claims that the same effect size or mechanism necessarily holds in tactical policing.
