## Extraction
A national survey of 2,733 police officers in Brazil examined officers' perceptions of their organizations' readiness for emergencies. Perceived readiness was related to trust in peers, managers and institutions, with nonlinear patterns also reported.

## Critical appraisal
Very strong locus proximity and direct evidence that police can rate organizational emergency readiness. It simultaneously shows a boundary problem for Ferry: perceived readiness is closely tied to trust/organizational conditions and is specific to emergency preparedness rather than a broad anticipation–coping–adaptation capability.
