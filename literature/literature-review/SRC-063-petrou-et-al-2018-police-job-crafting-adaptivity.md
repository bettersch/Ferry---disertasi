# SRC-063 — Petrou et al. (2018), job crafting and police adaptivity during change

## Identity and provenance
- SRC ID: SRC-063
- Track: literature-review (Stage 4 novelty audit)

- Source type: peer-reviewed three-wave longitudinal empirical article
- Metadata: Petrou, P., Demerouti, E., & Schaufeli, W. B. (2018). Crafting the Change: The Role of Employee Job Crafting Behaviors for Successful Organizational Change. *Journal of Management*.
- DOI: 10.1177/0149206315624961
- Access: FULL_TEXT via repository link returned by Elicit.

## Extraction
A three-wave study of 368 police officers linked organizational-change communication and job crafting to employee adjustment. Seeking job challenges was positively associated with adaptivity, while seeking resources was positively associated with work engagement.

## Critical appraisal
High-quality direct police evidence showing that challenge-seeking and resource processes can support adaptivity during change. It is not specifically about digital demands, POS, or Adaptive Performance as currently operationalized for Ferry.

## Statement relations
LIMITS novelty claims based on challenge/resource-to-adaptivity mechanisms in police; SUPPORTS the need to distinguish digital demand content and preserve engagement/job crafting as rival mechanisms.
