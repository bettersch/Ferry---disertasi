Search parameter sets: `PRM-002`, `PRM-004`. Screening parameter set: `PRM-003`. Search runs: `RUN-006`–`RUN-028`. Preferred window: 2021–2026 with explicit construct-anchor/closest-comparator exceptions.

## Synthesis patterns

| LIT | Pattern | Supporting sources | Counterexamples / qualifiers |
|---|---|---|---|
| LIT-001 | Specific digital work demands/stressors are repeatedly associated with exhaustion/burnout-related strain, but direct evidence using a distinct DJD construct in policing remains sparse. | SRC-012; SRC-013; SRC-020; SRC-021; SRC-022; SRC-028 | SRC-024 shows digital transition can be manageable/resource-supported. |
| LIT-002 | POS/organizational support has comparatively consistent evidence as a lower-burnout/lower-exhaustion resource in policing, but buffering is not universal. | SRC-014; SRC-018; SRC-019; SRC-012 | SRC-021 found no focal buffering by social support; support constructs differ. |
| LIT-003 | Earlier synthesis: direct Burnout→POR evidence not established and reverse/common-cause pathways plausible. | SRC-023; SRC-027; SRC-016; SRC-010 | **SUPERSEDED by LIT-006.** |
| LIT-004 | Employee-rated organizational resilience is feasible but construct definitions/levels vary and must be separated from readiness/agility/effectiveness/individual resilience. | SRC-010; SRC-016; SRC-024 | Updated by stronger measurement evidence in LIT-008. |
| LIT-005 | Digitalization can create employee-level demands while organization-level digital capabilities can strengthen adaptation/resilience. | SRC-020; SRC-022; SRC-024; SRC-025; SRC-026 | Different levels must not be collapsed. |
| LIT-006 | Organizational/collective adaptive or resilience conditions more often appear upstream of employee health than burnout appears upstream of employee-rated organizational resilience. | SRC-023; SRC-027; SRC-029; SRC-030; SRC-031; SRC-032; SRC-033 | Some evidence is adjacent rather than exact POR. |
| LIT-007 | Burnout/exhaustion can matter for performance/OCB/safety outcomes, but mixed/null findings do not establish Burnout→POR or a bottom-up OR mechanism. | SRC-030; SRC-034; SRC-036 | SRC-035 reports a null burnout–performance relation. |
| LIT-008 | Organizational-resilience measurement maturity has improved; inability to measure POR is no longer a sufficient rejection argument. | SRC-037; SRC-038 | Policing-specific validity and causal ordering remain unresolved. |
| LIT-009 | Readiness and agility are strong rivals with public-sector/police evidence, but readiness is change/emergency-specific and agility is typically an upstream organizational resource/capability. | SRC-029; SRC-039; SRC-040; SRC-041; SRC-044; SRC-045 | Neither is a neutral substitute for resilience or adaptive performance. |
| LIT-010 | Adaptive Performance has the cleanest person-level/measurement fit among downstream candidates, including Indonesian validation and public-sector evidence, while police-specific relation evidence remains limited. | SRC-042; SRC-043; SRC-046; SRC-001 | Outcome selection does not establish antecedent/mediation paths. |
