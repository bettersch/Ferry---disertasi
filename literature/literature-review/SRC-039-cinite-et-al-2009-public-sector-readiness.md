## Extraction
The instrument conceptualizes readiness around organizational conditions such as senior-management commitment, change-agent competence, manager support, communication and perceived consequences of change. This gives readiness mature public-sector precedent but also shows why it is not a neutral synonym for resilience: it is anchored to a focal change and overlaps conceptually with support/leadership/climate.

## Critical appraisal
High conceptual relevance for readiness as a rival outcome; not policing-specific and not evidence for a burnout-downstream mechanism.

## Statement relations
SUPPORTS readiness measurement maturity; LIMITS readiness as a broad resilience substitute; FLAGS overlap risk with POS/support constructs.
