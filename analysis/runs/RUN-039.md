## Purpose
Test whether digital demands can reasonably be assumed to have a uniformly negative relation with Adaptive Performance.

## Salient retrievals
- Chen et al. (2025): digital learning demands and digital workload produced different downstream behavioral processes.
- Li, Wang, & Wang (2026): technology requirements can be appraised as challenge or hindrance demands, with digital literacy shaping outcomes.
- Zhou et al. (2025): digital technology affordance can increase Adaptive Performance through job control while increasing threat through job demands for other employees.
- Abdul Hamid (2022): technology readiness positively related to Adaptive Performance.

## Interpretation
A universal negative DJD→Adaptive Performance claim is untenable. The unresolved performance-side problem concerns which digital-demand content is challenge-like versus hindrance-like, under what resource conditions, and whether the same demand domain can simultaneously increase strain while supporting or impairing adaptation.
