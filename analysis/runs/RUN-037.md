## Purpose
Test whether Adaptive Performance/adaptivity is genuinely absent from policing or already has a police/law-enforcement evidence base.

## Salient retrievals
- van den Heuvel, Demerouti, & Bakker (2014): 1-year follow-up of 580 police officers; resources predicted later adaptivity during organizational change.
- Oleszkiewicz et al. (2022): adaptability in a covert law-enforcement context.
- general Adaptive Performance reviews and public-sector measurement studies.

## Interpretation
Adaptive behavior in policing is not a blank literature. A novelty claim cannot be based on introducing adaptation/adaptivity to police. The remaining problem is narrower: how digital-domain demands and organizational support relate to a person-level adaptive-performance outcome under high-intensity modernization.
