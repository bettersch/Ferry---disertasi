## Purpose
Test whether resource/support→adaptivity mechanisms are absent from policing.

## Salient retrievals
- van den Heuvel et al. (2014): leader–member exchange and personal resources predicted later adaptivity among police officers during organizational change.
- Petrou, Demerouti, & Schaufeli (2018): three-wave police study linking change communication/job crafting to engagement and adaptivity.
- Kalyal & Grabarski (2020): organizational support-related HR practices linked to change-championing behavior among police officers.

## Interpretation
Police resource-to-adaptation mechanisms already exist. Therefore POS→Adaptive Performance in Ferry is not defensible as a wholly novel resource mechanism. The stronger contribution is integrating a precisely bounded digital-demand domain with a police-relevant resource and comparing their asymmetric implications for health impairment and adaptive performance.
