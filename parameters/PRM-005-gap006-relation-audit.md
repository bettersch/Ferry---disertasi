# PRM-005 — GAP-006 relation architecture audit

- Type: search parameter set
- Stage: 3
- Date: 2026-10-11
- Issue: #41
- Purpose: test relation-specific evidence among bounded DJD, POS, Burnout and individual Adaptive Performance without presupposing mediation.

## Evidence priorities

1. policing/public safety/high-reliability evidence;
2. public-sector evidence;
3. broader organizational evidence;
4. longitudinal, multilevel, multisource, supervisor-rated and meta-analytic evidence receive greater weight for directionality/performance claims than same-wave self-report models.

## Construct boundaries

- DJD must remain distinct from general workload, generic technostress responses and digital-skill deficits;
- POS must remain global organizational support rather than supervisor/coworker support;
- Burnout must remain distinct from stress/fatigue and single-dimension exhaustion, although exhaustion evidence may be used as a qualified mechanism comparator;
- Adaptive Performance must remain a person-level behavioral/performance domain rather than readiness, adaptability trait, general job performance or organizational resilience.

## Candidate architectures to test

- mediation: DJD/POS → Burnout → Adaptive Performance;
- parallel outcomes: DJD/POS → Burnout and DJD/POS → Adaptive Performance without causal ordering between outcomes;
- asymmetric architecture: retain only relations with sufficient direct evidence;
- rival motivational pathway: resources/demands → engagement/job crafting/self-efficacy → performance.

## Decision rule

No path is retained merely to preserve a four-variable SEM. Mediation requires stronger temporal/mechanistic support than simple association. Mixed challenge/hindrance evidence blocks a universal signed path unless the demand construct is correspondingly narrowed.

## Limits

Focused Elicit retrieval only; authenticated Scopus/Web of Science parity remains outstanding. Lack of an exact four-variable paper is not treated as a research gap or novelty.
