# PRM-006 — Stage 4 novelty audit

Issue: #44  
Date: 2026-10-11  
Purpose: test whether Ferry has a defensible novelty claim after GRO-03, without manufacturing novelty from a new location or exact variable combination.

## Candidate novelty classes
1. theoretical novelty: new mechanism/proposition or a meaningful boundary condition of JD-R/digital JD-R;
2. empirical novelty: an unresolved empirical pattern that matters theoretically;
3. construct/measurement novelty: sharper separation of bounded DJD from operational workload, digital-skill mismatch and generic technostress;
4. contextual extension: transferability to high-intensity policing/Korbrimob;
5. rejected pseudo-novelty: exact variable combination, country/locus alone, or focused-search non-retrieval.

## Search questions
- What digital-demand/technostress evidence already exists in policing?
- What Adaptive Performance/adaptivity evidence already exists in policing/law enforcement?
- Does the wider digital-work literature already show dual challenge/hindrance paths to performance/adaptation?
- Does policing already contain resource/support-to-adaptivity evidence?
- After accounting for these literatures, what problem remains substantively unresolved?

## Inclusion priorities
- police/law-enforcement/public-safety evidence where available;
- longitudinal/multiwave evidence preferred for directionality;
- peer-reviewed sources preferred;
- construct match: distinguish Adaptive Performance from generic job performance/adaptability traits, DJD from generic technostress or workload, and POS from generic support.

## Guardrails
- no claim of first/only study from bounded search results;
- no novelty from Korbrimob/Indonesia alone;
- no causal claim from cross-sectional studies;
- no reintroduction of Burnout mediation;
- no universal negative DJD→Adaptive Performance hypothesis;
- authenticated Scopus/Web of Science parity remains outstanding.

## Decision rule
Report GRO-04 only if the surviving novelty is more than contextual extension and can be expressed as a bounded theory/construct/empirical problem with explicit evidence and limitations.
