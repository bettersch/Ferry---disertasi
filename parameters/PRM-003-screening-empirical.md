# PRM-003 — Stage 3 empirical screening criteria

Status: ACTIVE / WORKING
Date: 2026-10-11
Issue: #29
Parent search parameter: `PRM-002-search-empirical.md`

## Inclusion criteria
1. Empirical journal article reporting an identifiable sample/corpus, design, and result.
2. Directly addresses at least one focal domain (bounded DJD/ICT demands/technostressors; POS; Burnout; POR/employee-rated organizational resilience) or one mandatory rival/falsification pathway.
3. Results are interpretable at the correct level of analysis; individual resilience is not treated as organizational resilience.
4. 2021–2026 is the preferred frontier window; pre-2021 work is included only when it is the closest comparator, canonical empirical source, or necessary contextual anchor.
5. Police/public-safety/high-reliability/public-sector evidence receives priority; adjacent sectors are retained when direct evidence is sparse and transfer limits are explicit.

## Exclusion criteria
1. Purely conceptual papers, editorials, conference proceedings, theses, and non-peer-reviewed reports when adequate journal evidence exists.
2. Studies that operationalize mere technology exposure/usage as a digital demand without a demand/strain distinction, unless retained as counterevidence.
3. Studies of individual/psychological resilience presented as if they measured organizational resilience.
4. Studies with no usable information about construct definition, sample/design, or empirical relation relevant to the Issue #29 architecture.
5. Duplicate reports from the same study unless the reports contribute non-overlapping evidence.

## Critical appraisal flags
- cross-sectional common-method design;
- same-source X–M–Y mediation without temporal ordering;
- construct conflation (technostress vs stress response; POS vs supervisor support; POR vs true OR; burnout vs exhaustion only);
- causal language stronger than design;
- weak locus transfer from generic white-collar/private-sector samples;
- journal/outlet or metadata uncertainty;
- contradictory/null effects hidden by aggregate conclusions.
