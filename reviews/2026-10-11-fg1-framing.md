## Why G2 can be READY without Scopus/Web of Science parity

The G2 criterion is whether the **review type and traceability match the coverage claimed**, not whether every project must use a named database. Ferry's registered review is explicitly a focused, iterative evidence reconstruction and counterevidence audit. Search parameters, screening rules, exact run queries/counts, source notes, appraisal limits, and contradictory/closest studies are recorded. The project explicitly refuses systematic/comprehensive, saturation, global absence, and first/only-study claims. Therefore missing authenticated Scopus/Web of Science parity is a material limitation and reopen trigger, but it is not a critical failure for the narrower framing claims currently made.

This judgment would change immediately if the dissertation later claims that the corpus is systematic/comprehensive, that no prior study exists, or that Ferry is the first/only study. Those stronger claims are not approved by this review.
