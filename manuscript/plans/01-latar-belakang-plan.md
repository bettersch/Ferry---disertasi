## Writing constraints

- No statement that DJD, POS, Burnout or POR is high/low in Korbrimob.
- No claim that digitalization causes burnout.
- No claim that burnout causes actual organizational resilience.
- No final hypothesis, mediation claim, method, instrument, estimator, novelty or contribution.
- Keep theory concise; detailed construct development belongs in Chapter II.
- Use the Renstra for field facts and peer-reviewed literature for construct/theory/empirical positioning.
