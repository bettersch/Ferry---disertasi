## Core problem architecture

The most defensible current problem is **not “digitalization causes burnout and lowers organizational resilience.”** The evidence supports a narrower and more coherent architecture:

> Korbrimob is modernizing technology-enabled, high-intensity operations while simultaneously managing capability, resource, leadership, welfare and organizational-adaptation demands. The dissertation should determine which employee-level work demands/resources are actually implicated in health impairment and whether any defensible downstream organizational-adaptation outcome follows.

This architecture is compatible with verified JD-R theory but does not preselect Digital Job Demands, POS, Burnout or Organizational Resilience as final variables.

## Reverse-direction and common-cause risks

Three causal-ordering risks must remain visible. First, a more resilient or better governed unit may provide healthier working conditions, so organizational adaptation could precede lower employee strain rather than result from it. Second, operational/crisis severity may simultaneously increase employee strain and reduce unit performance/adaptation. Third, staffing, leadership or resource scarcity may generate both higher demands and poorer organizational outcomes. None of these alternatives is resolved by the current documentary evidence.

## What evidence is still needed

Before construct lock, Issue #10 should ask only four practical questions: (1) are Digital Job Demands distinct from general operational demand and technology-capability mismatch in this locus; (2) is POS the best employee-level resource construct rather than objective resources or leadership/supervisor support; (3) is burnout the most defensible health-impairment construct for the target personnel; and (4) what outcome label and level best represent the organizational-adaptation domain.

## Ranked framing decision

1. **RETAIN:** high-intensity modernization under a demand–resource architecture.
2. **NARROW:** digital work demand as one candidate demand domain, not the whole problem.
3. **NARROW/DEFER:** POS until perception-level relevance is established.
4. **RETAIN as candidate mechanism, not field fact:** burnout.
5. **DEFER/NARROW:** Organizational Resilience pending level/outcome resolution.
6. **RETAIN as rival/common-cause set:** leadership, staffing and objective resource scarcity.

## Exit decision

The problem architecture is coherent enough to proceed to Issue #10 construct-fit audit. No construct, path, mediation, hypothesis, causal ordering or research gap is locked by this memo.
