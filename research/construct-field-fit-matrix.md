## Construct-specific judgments

### Digital Job Demands
DJD has the strongest direct field–theory alignment of the four candidates, but only after narrowing. The Renstra supports digital modernization and technology complexity, while the theory requires actual demand characteristics that consume sustained effort. Therefore the construct should not include mere technology use, modernization, or lack of digital skill.

### Perceived Organizational Support
The field clearly contains resource and welfare questions, but the evidence is mixed and objective. POS is defensible only if employee perceptions of organizational valuation/care are empirically salient. Leadership/supervisor support and objective resource sufficiency are credible alternatives and must remain visible.

### Burnout
Burnout is the strongest theoretically grounded candidate mechanism within JD-R, but the current institutional evidence does not establish it as the dominant Korbrimob strain outcome. It should remain available for Stage 3 empirical testing rather than be treated as a documented field fact.

### Organizational Resilience
The organizational-adaptation domain is clearly relevant, but the current label is structurally unresolved. The construct cannot be treated as an individual-level outcome without changing the claim or justifying aggregation/multilevel evidence. Issue #11 must decide the label and level before any final construct lock.

## Provisional result

- DJD: **NARROW**.
- POS: **DEFER**.
- Burnout: **DEFER**.
- Organizational Resilience: **DEFER to #11**.

This is not a rejection of the model. It is a coherence correction: only DJD currently has enough combined field/theory support to be narrowed positively, while the other three require specific evidence or structural resolution before lock.

## Exit decision

Proceed to Issue #11. No hypothesis, mediation path, causal direction, novelty, measurement instrument, method or estimator is approved by this audit.
