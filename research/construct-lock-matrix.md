## Lock semantics

`LOCK` in this document means **locked as a focal construct/domain for Stage 3 empirical-literature reconstruction**. It does not mean the construct is empirically prevalent at Korbrimob, that a path is causal, that mediation is established, or that a final instrument/method has been selected. Stage 3 may reopen a locked construct if direct empirical evidence, counterevidence, construct overlap, or a stronger rival makes the current choice untenable.

## Revised working problem statement

Korbrimob's 2025–2029 strategic context combines high-intensity specialized operations with rapid technology-enabled modernization, capability-development needs, mixed resource/support conditions, leadership/governance weaknesses, and explicit organizational-adaptation targets. The research problem is therefore not whether digitization is harmful by definition, but whether a **bounded employee demand–resource configuration**—provisionally represented by Digital Job Demands and Perceived Organizational Support—is meaningfully associated with employee health impairment (Burnout) and with personnel perceptions of the organization's adaptive/resilience capacity (Perceived Organizational Resilience), after serious rival explanations are considered.

This is a problem architecture, not a causal conclusion.

## Working Stage 3 association architecture

**Demand domain:** narrowed Digital Job Demands  
**Resource domain:** Perceived Organizational Support  
**Health-impairment domain:** Burnout  
**Downstream perception domain:** Perceived Organizational Resilience

The conceptual ordering `demand/resource → burnout → POR` is retained only as the **primary literature-audit sequence derived from JD-R and the prior working model**. Direct paths, reverse direction, common causes, mediation, and causal effects remain open questions. Stage 3 must not screen literature as though this ordering were already proven.

## Mandatory rival searches for Stage 3

| Locked domain | Rivals/boundaries that must be searched alongside it |
|---|---|
| DJD | general workload/operational demands; technology intensity; digital competence/capability mismatch; technostress stressors/resources |
| POS | supervisor/leadership support; objective organizational resources; welfare/benefits; organizational/support climate |
| Burnout | fatigue/exhaustion; trauma-related strain/PTSD where relevant; general psychological distress; technostrain |
| POR | organizational readiness; agility; adaptive performance/capability; operational effectiveness; service continuity; true organizational resilience |

Relation-specific review must also retain reverse-direction and common-cause evidence involving leadership, staffing, resource scarcity and crisis/operational severity.

## What remains unknown

- whether the locked constructs have adequate direct empirical support in policing/high-reliability settings;
- which DJD domains are relevant to Korbrimob work rather than imported from white-collar contexts;
- whether POS is more explanatory than supervisor support or objective resources;
- whether burnout is sufficiently salient and discriminable in the intended population;
- whether POR is psychometrically distinct from POS, readiness/agility and general organizational-effectiveness perceptions;
- the strongest empirical ordering among Burnout and POR;
- measurement instruments, sampling/access, paradigm, method, estimator, hypotheses, novelty and contribution.

## GO / NO-GO decision

**GO — Stage 3 empirical literature reconstruction may begin.**

Reason: the focal constructs are now sufficiently bounded to generate reproducible construct-specific and relation-specific searches, while the rival constructs and falsification routes are explicit enough to prevent a purely confirmatory search. `GO` does not mean FG1 is passed and does not authorize GRO-03 until the Stage 3 evidence synthesis establishes what the empirical literature actually supports.
