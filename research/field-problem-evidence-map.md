# Renstra-grounded Korbrimob field/problem evidence map

Status: Stage 1 / pre-Stage-3 field alignment evidence
Primary source: `SRC-001` — Renstra Korps Brimob Polri 2025–2029, KEP/299/V/2025, 23 May 2025
Purpose: establish what the institutional field actually documents before candidate constructs are locked.

## Evidence boundary

`SRC-001` is the mandatory primary institutional guideline for locus/problem alignment (DEC-014). It can establish institutional conditions, weaknesses, priorities, responses and targets. It cannot by itself establish employee-level Digital Job Demands, Perceived Organizational Support, burnout, Organizational Resilience scores, causal effects, mediation or novelty.

## Stale / bounded statistics

The Renstra's SWOT includes a police-to-population ratio using 2020 population data while discussing the 2025–2029 period (PDF p. 26). This can support historical staffing-context discussion only; it should not be presented as a current 2026 population/personnel estimate without independent verification.

## Coherence synthesis

The institutional evidence supports a coherent field picture: Korbrimob operates in a high-intensity specialized environment while pursuing technology-enabled modernization, personnel capability development, more effective performance systems and organizational adaptation. At the same time, the Renstra records capability gaps, operational-resource constraints, unmet welfare needs, leadership/governance weaknesses and modernization requirements. These conditions are not reducible to one digital problem.

The strongest next-step framing therefore should not ask whether the four current constructs can simply be attached to the Renstra. Issue #9 should discriminate among four competing explanations: **digital demand versus general operational demand/capability mismatch; perceived support versus objective resource and leadership conditions; burnout versus other forms of operational strain; and Organizational Resilience versus readiness/agility/operational effectiveness.**

## Decision from Issue #8

No construct is locked. `SRC-001` establishes field relevance and competing problem domains only. The evidence is sufficient to proceed to Issue #9 problem-architecture audit without claiming a scholarly gap, causal pathway or final outcome construct.
