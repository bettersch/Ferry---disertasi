## Current Stage 3 architecture

The working architecture is **parallel/asymmetric**, not a burnout-mediation chain.

- **DJD–Burnout:** retained as a theory-consistent positive association, with policing-specific DJD boundaries still under-evidenced.
- **POS–Burnout:** retained as the strongest police-specific resource/health relation.
- **POS–Adaptive Performance:** retained as a qualified positive association; direct police evidence remains limited.
- **Burnout–Adaptive Performance:** retained as a qualified adverse association based on broader meta-analytic/performance and police evidence; Burnout is not locked as mediator.
- **DJD–Adaptive Performance:** direction remains open because digital/job demands may function as challenge or hindrance demands depending on content/appraisal/resources.

Under `DEC-018`–`DEC-020`, **Burnout mediation is not restored**. Adaptive Performance is the working person-level performance/adaptation outcome. POR, readiness and agility remain contextual/rival adaptation domains.

General workload, digital capability mismatch, leadership, staffing, objective resources, trust/safety climate, work engagement/job crafting and self-efficacy remain important rival mechanisms or conditions; none is added as a focal construct at this stage.

## Stage 4 novelty and RQ position

The Stage 4 counterevidence audit deliberately tested and rejected inflated novelty claims. Direct policing evidence already covers technology difficulty/support and well-being, police-change research already covers resource/challenge processes and adaptivity, and broader digital-work research already demonstrates differentiated digital demands and double-edged performance/adaptation consequences.

The live gap is `GAP-007`: the inspected corpus does not yet establish which digital-demand contents in high-intensity policing are distinct from ordinary operational workload and digital-skill mismatch, or whether those bounded demands show asymmetric relations with Burnout and Adaptive Performance alongside POS.

`NOV-001` positions the dissertation provisionally as a **theory-boundary + construct-clarification + empirical-pattern test** of digital JD-R. GRO-04 is researcher-verified. `DEC-022` translates that direction into three reported questions: construct boundary (`RQ-001`), health-impairment relations (`RQ-002`) and Adaptive Performance relations (`RQ-003`).

Directional expectations are limited to DJD–Burnout (+), POS–Burnout (−), POS–Adaptive Performance (+) and Burnout–Adaptive Performance (−). DJD–Adaptive Performance remains exploratory/open. No mediation or moderation is introduced merely to increase model complexity.
