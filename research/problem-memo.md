## 1. Phenomenon (Stage 1)

Korbrimob Polri is operating under a 2025–2029 strategic direction that combines continued responsibility for high-intensity public-safety operations with increasing technology-based operations, data analytics, and personnel adaptation to more complex information technology. The Renstra also identifies limitations in mastery of communication technology as an internal personnel-capability issue. This establishes a **digital-modernization and adaptation phenomenon at the locus**. It does **not** establish that Digital Job Demands are high, that digitalization causes burnout, or that organizational resilience is low.

Statements: `CTX-001`, `PHN-001`, `PHN-002`. Evidence: `SRC-001`.

## 3. Main problem

### Current researchable problem boundary

The locus evidence shows an organizational transition: Korbrimob is expected to maintain high-intensity operational capability while increasing the role of technology, data analytics, and technology-related personnel capability. What remains scientifically unexplained is **which employee-level and/or organizational conditions produced by or relevant to this transition are sufficiently distinct, measurable, and theoretically meaningful to warrant a doctoral-level explanatory study**.

The current working title proposes Digital Job Demands, Perceived Organizational Support, Burnout, and Organizational Resilience. These are retained as **candidate concepts from prior project work**, not as final variables. Their role must be re-earned through theory and empirical-literature review.

### Unit and level of analysis

- Candidate population/locus: Korbrimob Polri personnel.
- Final unit of analysis: **DECISION NEEDED**.
- Particular risk: Organizational Resilience is ordinarily an organizational-level capability, whereas burnout and perceived work conditions are individual-level constructs. If the study relies only on individual survey responses, the project must determine whether the defensible outcome is employee-rated/perceived organizational resilience rather than actual organizational-level resilience.

### Explicitly outside the current framing claim

- No claim that Korbrimob performance or resilience is low.
- No claim that personnel have high Digital Job Demands or high burnout.
- No causal claim from digitalization to burnout/resilience.
- No method, estimator, mediation structure, or measurement instrument is selected here.
