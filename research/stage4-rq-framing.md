## 1. Framing rule

The questions must preserve the novelty that survived the counterevidence audit. Ferry is not asking whether four variables can be placed in one model. The study is asking whether a **bounded digital job-demand domain** can be distinguished from ordinary operational workload and digital-skill mismatch, and whether that domain shows different associations with **Burnout** and **Adaptive Performance** alongside **Perceived Organizational Support (POS)** in high-intensity policing.

The wording therefore remains associative rather than causal. No mediator, moderator, estimator, instrument, sampling plan, or design is locked here.

## 2. Proposed research questions

### RQ-001 — construct boundary
**Sejauh mana Tuntutan Kerja Digital yang relevan bagi personel Korbrimob Polri dapat dibedakan secara empiris dari beban kerja operasional umum dan ketidakcocokan keterampilan digital?**

Reason: `GAP-007` and `NOV-001` are not defensible if the study merely relabels ordinary workload or digital-skill mismatch as DJD. Construct separation is therefore a substantive prerequisite, not a technical afterthought.

### RQ-002 — health-impairment domain
**Bagaimana hubungan Tuntutan Kerja Digital yang telah dibatasi secara konseptual dan Perceived Organizational Support dengan Burnout pada personel Korbrimob Polri?**

Reason: JD-R provides the health-impairment logic; the empirical literature supports a positive DJD/digital-demand–strain relation and a comparatively consistent negative POS–Burnout relation.

### RQ-003 — adaptive-performance domain
**Bagaimana hubungan Tuntutan Kerja Digital yang telah dibatasi secara konseptual, Perceived Organizational Support, dan Burnout dengan Adaptive Performance pada personel Korbrimob Polri?**

Reason: the performance/adaptation side is the critical asymmetric boundary. POS has a qualified positive association with Adaptive Performance, Burnout has a qualified adverse performance association, but the sign of DJD–Adaptive Performance remains open because challenge/hindrance evidence is mixed.

## 3. Directional hypotheses and exploratory relation

These are **association hypotheses**, not causal-effect claims. They remain provisional until GRO-05 is verified and Stage 5 determines whether the design can support them.

- **H1:** Tuntutan Kerja Digital yang telah dibatasi secara konseptual berhubungan positif dengan Burnout.
- **H2:** Perceived Organizational Support berhubungan negatif dengan Burnout.
- **H3:** Perceived Organizational Support berhubungan positif dengan Adaptive Performance.
- **H4:** Burnout berhubungan negatif dengan Adaptive Performance.
- **Exploratory DJD–Adaptive Performance:** no signed hypothesis is set. The study should estimate/test the association and interpret its direction only after the relevant DJD content is bounded and the design/measurement is fixed.

No Burnout mediation hypothesis is restored. No POS moderation hypothesis is introduced. Challenge/hindrance appraisal, work engagement, job crafting, leadership, digital literacy/capability, staffing, objective resources, shift/mission intensity and operational workload remain rival mechanisms or conditions rather than automatic additions to the focal model.

## 4. Construct-boundary requirement

RQ-001 means the later measurement/design stage must demonstrate that the selected DJD content is not merely:

- general operational workload;
- generic technostress response;
- technology difficulty or low technology efficacy;
- digital literacy/competence deficit;
- burnout/exhaustion itself.

This does not predetermine a specific psychometric method. It sets a validity requirement: structural relations should not be interpreted until the DJD domain has defensible content and discriminant boundaries.

## 5. Contribution statement proposed for the brain

**CTB-001:** Penelitian Ferry berkontribusi dengan menguji batas penerapan digital JD-R pada high-intensity policing melalui pembedaan tuntutan kerja digital dari beban operasional umum dan ketidakcocokan keterampilan digital serta pengujian pola hubungan asimetrisnya dengan Burnout dan Adaptive Performance bersama POS sebagai sumber daya organisasi yang dipersepsikan.

This is a bounded contribution claim. It does not claim a new grand theory, a first/only study, causal effects, or novelty from Korbrimob/Indonesia alone.

## 6. Promised implications

### Theoretical
The study promises to clarify for JD-R/digital-JD-R scholarship whether digital-demand content needs to be separated more explicitly when health impairment and Adaptive Performance are considered together, rather than assuming one uniformly harmful digital-demand pathway.

### Practical
For Korbrimob leadership/HR, the study promises evidence capable of distinguishing digital-specific demands associated with strain from support conditions associated with adaptive functioning, subject to the final design and inferential limits. It does not promise that a particular intervention will work.

## 7. Rival explanations and falsification

The framing should be revised if:

- the selected DJD items cannot be distinguished from ordinary operational workload or skill mismatch;
- the observed performance pattern is better explained by work engagement, job crafting, self-efficacy/digital literacy, leadership or staffing than by the focal architecture;
- RQ-003 is forced into a universal negative DJD→Adaptive Performance claim despite challenge/hindrance counterevidence;
- Burnout mediation is reintroduced only to create model complexity;
- later design choices cannot answer RQ-001 while preserving the claimed construct-clarification contribution.
