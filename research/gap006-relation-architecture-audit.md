# GAP-006 relation architecture audit

Status: `RESOLVED-PARALLEL-ASYMMETRIC`
Issue: #41
Date: 2026-10-11
Primary field guideline: `SRC-001` Renstra Korbrimob Polri 2025–2029
Search basis: `PRM-005`, `RUN-029`–`RUN-035`, existing Stage 3 evidence, and `SRC-048`–`SRC-056`

## 1. Decision problem

After `DEC-019`, the four working domains are bounded Digital Job Demands (DJD), Perceived Organizational Support (POS), Burnout, and individual Adaptive Performance. The question is whether these domains form a defensible mediation chain or whether the evidence supports a simpler demand–resource architecture with distinct health and performance outcomes.

The audit therefore tested each relation separately and gave greater weight to police/public-safety evidence, temporal designs, meta-analytic evidence and construct match.

## 2. Relation-by-relation findings

### DJD ↔ Burnout — RETAIN, bounded

This remains the strongest role for DJD. Existing digital-work evidence (`SRC-020`, `SRC-021`, `SRC-022`, `SRC-028`) repeatedly links specific ICT/technology demands with exhaustion, technostrain or burnout-related outcomes. Police longitudinal JD-R evidence (`SRC-048`) independently supports the general pattern that increasing demands and low/decreasing resources accompany increasing burnout.

The qualification remains important: police-specific studies do not yet cleanly isolate the exact bounded DJD construct from general operational workload and digital-skill mismatch (`GAP-003`). Thus the retained claim is an **association/theory-consistent pathway**, not proof that Korbrimob digitalization causes burnout.

### POS ↔ Burnout — RETAIN

This remains the best-supported police-specific relation. `SRC-014`, `SRC-018`, `SRC-019` and the broader JD-R police evidence support lower burnout/exhaustion/stress under higher organizational support/resources. The effect should not be generalized into a universal moderation/buffering claim because support source and model specification matter.

### POS ↔ Adaptive Performance — RETAIN AS WORKING PERFORMANCE RELATION

Direct Adaptive Performance studies support a positive relation between organizational support/POS and adaptive behavior/performance (`SRC-052`, `SRC-053`), while the wider POS literature supports employee-performance consequences. The mechanisms in the strongest Adaptive Performance studies are motivational and capability-related—job crafting, work engagement, role-breadth self-efficacy—rather than burnout.

The limitation is transferability: direct police-specific POS→Adaptive Performance evidence was not identified in the focused run. Therefore the relation is suitable for Stage 3/GRO framing but remains to be tested in the Korbrimob context rather than treated as established locally.

### Burnout ↔ Adaptive Performance — RETAIN AS QUALIFIED ASSOCIATION, NOT MEDIATOR

Evidence supports the proposition that burnout/resource depletion can impair performance, but the construct match varies. A meta-analysis (`SRC-051`) reports modest negative burnout–job-performance associations. Police evidence includes a large negative burnout–performance association (`SRC-050`) and a three-wave study in which emotional exhaustion helped explain an anxiety→performance relation (`SRC-049`). An exact general-demand→burnout→Adaptive Performance study exists (`SRC-056`), but it is cross-sectional, non-police and uses general job demands.

The evidence therefore supports a **plausible negative association between Burnout and Adaptive Performance**, but not a sufficiently identified mediation mechanism for Ferry.

### DJD ↔ Adaptive Performance — DO NOT LOCK A DIRECTION

This is the least stable relation. Direct exact evidence is sparse, while adjacent evidence shows mixed technology-demand/performance patterns. Longitudinal digital-transformation evidence (`SRC-054`) demonstrates that technology requirements can function as challenge or hindrance demands depending on appraisal/resources. Broader longitudinal job-demand evidence (`SRC-055`) likewise shows that some challenge demands can relate positively to later performance.

Consequently, treating all DJD as a uniformly negative predictor of Adaptive Performance would be theoretically and empirically premature. The relation can remain an empirical question, but its sign should not be precommitted before the relevant DJD dimensions and appraisal conditions are established.

## 3. Mediation audit

**Burnout mediation is not restored.**

Three reasons are decisive:

1. exact DJD→Burnout→Adaptive Performance evidence is too thin and mostly non-police/cross-sectional;
2. the JD-R literature gives a serious rival mechanism in which job resources relate to performance through work engagement/motivation rather than burnout (`SRC-052`, `RUN-033`);
3. digital demands can have challenge/hindrance properties, so a single universal indirect effect through burnout would hide theoretically meaningful heterogeneity.

A statistical indirect effect found later in cross-sectional data would not, by itself, establish a causal mediation mechanism.

## 4. Preferred working architecture

**`GAP-006 = RESOLVED-PARALLEL-ASYMMETRIC`.**

Use a **parallel/asymmetric demand–resource architecture**, not a single mediation chain:

- **Health-impairment domain:** DJD is retained as a candidate demand associated with higher Burnout; POS is retained as a resource associated with lower Burnout.
- **Adaptive-performance domain:** POS is retained as a candidate positive correlate/antecedent of Adaptive Performance; DJD→Adaptive Performance remains open in direction because challenge/hindrance differentiation matters.
- **Cross-outcome relation:** Burnout–Adaptive Performance is retained as a theoretically plausible negative association, but Burnout is **not locked as mediator** between DJD/POS and Adaptive Performance.

Burnout and Adaptive Performance should therefore be treated as distinct person-level outcomes/process domains rather than forcing Adaptive Performance to be the terminal node of a burnout mediation chain.

## 5. Rival mechanisms that remain live

- work engagement and job crafting as motivational/resource mechanisms linking organizational resources to performance (`SRC-052`, `RUN-033`);
- role-breadth self-efficacy/personal resources in POS→Adaptive Performance (`SRC-053`);
- digital literacy/capability and challenge–hindrance appraisal in digital-demand effects (`SRC-054`);
- general operational workload, leadership, staffing, trust/safety climate and objective resources in policing/high-reliability settings;
- reverse/reciprocal demand–burnout dynamics demonstrated in longitudinal JD-R research.

These rivals should shape later measurement/control/design decisions but are **not added as new focal constructs at this stage**.

## 6. Implication for the research gap

The dissertation gap should **not** be stated as “no study combines DJD, POS, Burnout and Adaptive Performance.” The defensible unresolved problem is narrower:

> Under high-intensity policing modernization, the evidence base does not yet establish how a **bounded digital job-demand domain**, distinct from general operational workload and digital-skill mismatch, relates alongside **perceived organizational support** to both employee health impairment and adaptive performance; the performance-side direction of digital demands is especially contingent on challenge/hindrance characteristics.

This is presently an inference from a focused corpus and must remain qualified by the absence of authenticated Scopus/Web of Science parity.

## 7. GRO-03 checkpoint

**GO TO REPORT GRO-03, WITH QUALIFICATION.**

There is now enough coherence to report the literature direction to the researcher because:

- the unsupported POR mediation has been removed;
- the downstream outcome is level-consistent;
- the retained relations are separated by strength rather than forced into a symmetric SEM;

GRO-03 must explicitly state that the direct DJD→Adaptive Performance direction is unresolved and that mediation is not established. Search saturation and Scopus/WoS parity are not claimed.

## 8. Architecture summary

**Strongly retained for framing:**
- DJD ↔ Burnout (positive theory-consistent association; exact police DJD still under-bounded)
- POS ↔ Burnout (negative association)

**Retained with qualification:**
- POS ↔ Adaptive Performance (positive association; direct police evidence limited)
- Burnout ↔ Adaptive Performance (likely negative/modest; adaptive-specific police evidence limited)

**Open direction:**
- DJD ↔ Adaptive Performance

**Rejected as model lock:**
- Burnout mediation of DJD/POS → Adaptive Performance
- any universal claim that digital demands necessarily reduce Adaptive Performance
