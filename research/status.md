## Verified theory and field architecture

JD-R remains the primary grand theory. The retained field problem is **high-intensity modernization under a demand–resource configuration**, not digitization-as-harm by assumption. Renstra remains mandatory institutional guidance and documents serious rival explanations involving operational intensity, capability mismatch, leadership, staffing, welfare/resources and organizational performance/adaptation.

## Stage 3 focused empirical result

- **Digital Job Demands:** retain in bounded form. Digital-demand/technostress evidence supports a health-impairment relation, but a clean policing-specific DJD construct separated from operational workload and digital-skill mismatch remains under-evidenced.
- **Perceived Organizational Support:** retain. Police evidence for lower burnout/exhaustion is comparatively strong; universal buffering should not be assumed.
- **Burnout:** retain as focal health-impairment construct. Direct Brimob and police evidence supports contextual relevance, but prevalence in Korbrimob is not established.
- **Perceived Organizational Resilience:** retain cautiously as a person-level perception domain; construct/measurement distinctness from readiness, agility, effectiveness and POS remains unresolved.
- **Burnout → POR / mediation:** revise. The focused corpus does not establish this path and includes credible reverse-direction evidence in which resilient/adaptive organizational conditions are associated with lower exhaustion/burnout.
- **Digitalization → true OR:** retain as mandatory counterpath. Digital capabilities can strengthen organization-level adaptation/resilience.
