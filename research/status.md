## GAP-004 resolution

**`RESOLVED-REVISE-MODEL`.**

The targeted directionality audit (`RUN-012`–`RUN-017`) did not identify direct evidence sufficient to lock Burnout as an antecedent or mediator of POR. The stronger pattern inspected places adaptive/resilient organizational or collective conditions upstream of employee health, and high-reliability studies also support common organizational antecedents such as climate, leadership and trust. Burnout can still affect performance-, OCB-, or safety-relevant behavior, but that is not evidence for Burnout→POR.

The absence of a direct Burnout→POR study in the bounded searches is **not** itself treated as novelty or proof of literature absence.
