## Current theory and field architecture

JD-R remains the verified primary grand theory. The field problem is high-intensity modernization under a demand–resource configuration, not digitalization-as-harm by assumption. Renstra remains mandatory field guidance but does not establish DJD, POS, Burnout or Adaptive Performance levels or causal relations.

## Current construct/relation architecture

- **Digital Job Demands:** retained only in bounded form; must be distinguished from operational workload, generic technostress/technology difficulty and digital-skill mismatch.
- **Perceived Organizational Support:** retained as focal employee-perceived resource.
- **Burnout:** retained as focal health-impairment outcome/domain.
- **Adaptive Performance:** retained as focal person-level adaptation/performance outcome.
- **DJD–Burnout:** retained as theory-consistent positive association.
- **POS–Burnout:** strongest police-specific resource/health relation; negative association.
- **POS–Adaptive Performance:** retained with qualification; direct police evidence remains limited.
- **Burnout–Adaptive Performance:** retained only as a qualified adverse association, not as an identified mediator.
- **DJD–Adaptive Performance:** sign remains open because digital demands can be challenge-like or hindrance-like.
- **Burnout mediation:** not restored.

## Stage 4 novelty audit

Issue #44 tested the novelty against counterevidence rather than preserving the historical model.

Counterevidence shows:
- police technology difficulty/support/stress relationships already exist (`SRC-057`);
- a recent PRESISI-linked public-sector study already combines technostress, burnout/engagement and generic performance (`SRC-058`);
- digital JD-R and differentiated digital-demand content already exist (`SRC-059`, `SRC-060`);
- digitalization already has double-edged Adaptive Performance implications outside policing (`SRC-061`);
- police resources/challenge seeking/job crafting already predict adaptivity in longitudinal change research (`SRC-062`, `SRC-063`).

Therefore novelty is **not**:
- a new grand theory;
- an exact four-variable combination;
- Korbrimob/Indonesia as a new setting;
- a generic technostress→burnout→performance chain;
- a universal negative DJD→Adaptive Performance path.

## Live gap and novelty direction

`GAP-003` has been refined into `GAP-007`:

> The inspected literature does not yet establish which digital-demand contents in high-intensity policing are distinct from ordinary operational workload and digital-skill mismatch, or whether those bounded digital demands have asymmetric relations with Burnout and Adaptive Performance alongside POS.

`NOV-001` therefore positions the study provisionally as a **theory-boundary + construct-clarification + empirical-pattern test** of digital JD-R in high-intensity policing. The contribution is to resolve a meaningful tension between resource depletion/health impairment and adaptation/performance rather than to claim a missing variable combination.
